# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The general public — visitors who land on labs.dreamfluid.studio to see what Dreamfluid is building, plus anyone who directly uses a published tool. Secondary audience: prospective collaborators/clients gauging Dreamfluid's creative and technical capability. Dreamfluid's own team also uses tools hosted here as part of internal R&D.

## Product Purpose

A public site that hosts information and tools for Dreamfluid Labs: creative tools, experiments, and R&D output from Dreamfluid. It exists so that Dreamfluid's experimental work has a real, shippable home instead of staying internal. Success means tools actually ship, get used, and the site reads as a credible record of Dreamfluid's experimental capability — not a placeholder.

## Positioning

Dreamfluid's brand thesis splits into two halves: "the substance of dreams" (Dreamfluid Studios and Dreamfluid Music — narrative, cinematic output) and "the fuel for creativity" (Dreamfluid Technology and, centrally, Dreamfluid Labs — the engine itself). Labs is not backstage support for Studios' public output; it is the literal "engine for vision" — Dreamfluid's central R&D, experimentation, and incubation hub, building the internal and public-facing creative tools that power creation across every division. It is allowed to look, feel, and move differently from Dreamfluid Studios' narrative/cinematic brand voice: rougher, faster, more technical/lab-coded, while still being publicly attributed to Dreamfluid.

## Operating Context

Currently: a minimal landing page (`/`) and a `/tools` index page. `/tools` is presently a placeholder ("Nothing here yet") — it previously hosted a BILT subscription tracker, a public-facing utility that was built, shipped, and later removed (2026-03-24). Built with Next.js 15 / React 19 / Tailwind CSS, deployed on Vercel.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Tools published here will be a mix of:
- internal R&D tools/experiments that support Dreamfluid's own creative process (not necessarily meant for public use), and
- standalone public-facing utilities anyone can use directly (as BILT tracker was).

No committed list of specific upcoming tools yet — this mix is confirmed, individual tools are not.

## Evidence on Hand

No testimonials, case studies, or press exist yet — do not fabricate any. The BILT subscription tracker is evidence Labs has shipped and iterated on a real public tool before, but it was removed and should not be referenced as a current/live demo.

## Product Principles

1. Ship real, usable tools rather than a portfolio of intentions — an empty "Tools" index is a gap to close, not a resting state.
2. Labs is allowed to diverge visually from the parent Dreamfluid Studios brand in service of feeling experimental, technical, and fast-moving.
3. Serve two audiences at once without compromising either: a tool must work for whoever uses it directly, while the site as a whole must read as credible R&D output to outside visitors.
4. Internal R&D tools and public-facing utilities are both legitimate content here — don't force every experiment to pretend it's a polished consumer product.
